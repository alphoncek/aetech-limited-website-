Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about our services

Which services can I currently request?

ÆTeCH currently lists three services as available within a limited scope: basic website creation and small software projects; basic data preparation, analysis and reporting; and basic troubleshooting and technical assistance. Contact us to confirm whether we can accommodate your specific request.

Are all 13 services ready for customers?

No. Three services are listed as available within the stated limited scope, five are in development and five are planned. Services in development or planned should not be treated as generally available offerings.

Is ÆTeCH AI production-ready?

ÆTeCH AI is in engineering and pre-production development. We do not represent it as production-ready. Readiness depends on completing appropriate testing, security, reliability, deployment and operational checks.

Can I engage ÆTeCH for data governance or AI compliance work?

Advanced Data Governance & Responsible AI is currently a planned service. ÆTeCH is not yet advertising it as an available consulting or compliance service. Organisations seeking immediate compliance advice should consult an appropriately qualified professional. We intend to develop responsible AI and data governance capabilities as our technical capacity grows.

Does ÆTeCH provide AI compliance consulting?

Not currently as an advertised available service. Advanced Data Governance & Responsible AI remains planned. Organisations needing immediate AI compliance advice should consult an appropriately qualified professional.
